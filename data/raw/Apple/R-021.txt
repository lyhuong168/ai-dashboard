Apple to Announce Fiscal Q4 2026 Earnings on November 2 

 Chris Hauk - Oct 6, 2026 

Apple today announced that it plans to report its earnings results for the fourth fiscal quarter (third calendar quarter) of 2026 on Monday, November 2, 2026 at 2:00 p.m. PT / 5:00 p.m. ET.

New Apple CEO John Ternus and CFO Kevan Parekh will discuss the results during a 2:00 p.m. Pacific Time conference call, which Apple will stream on its website.

A live audio stream of the call will be available on Apple’s Investor Relations page, and a recording will be available later in the day for replay.

Apple’s Q4 2026 earnings information will cover launch-week iPhone 18 Pro sales. Apple also released the Apple Watch Series 12, Apple Watch Ultra 4, and AirPods 5 during the same quarter.

Memory pricing and supply constraints, as well as holiday quarter expectations will likely be brought up during the call.

Q4 2025 revenue was $102.5 billion, so Apple’s guidance suggests an increase of $111.7 to $113.7 billion for Q4 2026.
